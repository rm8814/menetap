# Menetap Phase 0 — Validation Brief

## Status

**Phase 0 status: In execution — strategy defined, recruitment preparation complete.**

Last updated: 2026-09-25

## Confirmed decisions

### Geographic rollout

1. Greater Yogyakarta
2. Java Island: West Java, Central Java, East Java, and Greater Jakarta
3. Bali
4. Indonesia overall

### Initial property coverage

Menetap will initially support:

- Independent hotels
- Boutique hotels
- Budget hotels
- Midscale hotels
- Upscale hotels
- Villas
- Guesthouses
- Homestays

Apartments are intentionally excluded from the initial scope.

### Initial inventory model

Hotels and properties will be onboarded through:

- Self-onboarding
- Assisted onboarding by the Menetap/UPSCALE team

Initial scope does not include PMS, channel-manager, or RMS integrations.

### Initial guest promise

Menetap will lead with:

- Transparent, competitive pricing
- A better stay experience
- Curated listings

### Pilot size and coverage

- Target **15–20 properties** in the first pilot.
- Cover the broader Greater Yogyakarta area rather than limiting the pilot to one neighborhood.

### Initial stay extensions

The first add-on service set will include:

- Airport shuttle
- Train transfer
- Breakfast
- Early check-in
- Late check-out
- Travel insurance

### Property curation criteria

A property should meet the following standards before being published:

- Verified ownership or management
- Accurate photos and listing information
- Reliable availability updates
- Transparent total pricing
- Acceptable cleanliness and service standards
- Responsive partner communication
- Good guest reviews or satisfactory inspection results

### Initial commercial model

- Base hotel commission: **10%**.
- Property-specific commission overrides are allowed.
- Featured properties receive an additional **2%** commission.
- Final treatment of taxes, payment processing fees, discounts, and add-on commissions remains to be defined.

### Pilot operating model

- Menetap support owns all guest communication.
- Hotels handle hotel-related requests and property fulfillment.
- Shuttle and train-transfer providers handle transport fulfillment.
- UPSCALE operations handles escalations and cross-party issues.

### Guest acquisition

Initial acquisition channels:

- Direct digital marketing
- Social media
- Content marketing

### Pilot success threshold

The first pilot is considered commercially launched when **20 properties are live** and available for booking.

### Definition of a live property

A property is considered live only when all of the following are complete:

- Property approved and verified
- Listing published
- At least one room type and rate available
- Availability updated for the next 30 days
- Partner trained on reservations and guest requests
- Cancellation and payment terms configured

### Initial payment model

- Primary payment method: **pay at hotel**.
- Alternative payment method: **manual bank transfer handled by Menetap**.
- Payment gateway integration is out of scope for the MVP.
- Payment status and bank-transfer reconciliation must still be recorded operationally.

### Launch timeline

- Target: **30 days** to launch the first 20 live properties.

Suggested 30-day execution sequence:

#### Days 1–5 — Prepare

- Finalize partner pitch and onboarding materials.
- Finalize partner agreement, commission terms, cancellation rules, and payment instructions.
- Prepare listing template, property verification checklist, and training guide.
- Create a target list of at least 25–30 prospective properties to allow for drop-off.
- Define the first Greater Yogyakarta coverage plan.

#### Days 6–15 — Recruit and verify

- Begin direct outreach to target properties.
- Conduct onboarding calls and property qualification.
- Verify ownership or management contact.
- Collect property data, room details, rates, availability, policies, photos, and service information.
- Identify transport and breakfast fulfillment providers.

#### Days 16–23 — Publish and train

- Review and approve listings.
- Publish qualified properties.
- Configure at least one room type and rate per property.
- Load the next 30 days of availability.
- Configure cancellation and payment terms.
- Train partners on reservations, guest requests, and escalation procedures.
- Run test booking scenarios with each property.

#### Days 24–30 — Soft launch

- Confirm 20 properties meet the live-property definition.
- Publish launch content and social media campaigns.
- Start direct digital acquisition.
- Monitor every booking and guest request manually.
- Track listing quality, availability accuracy, response times, and booking issues.
- Hold a daily launch review with Menetap support and UPSCALE operations.

## Current strategic interpretation

The first version should be a curated, operationally supported marketplace rather than an inventory-volume competitor. Menetap can differentiate through the quality of listings, pricing clarity, direct partner relationships, and support around the stay.
