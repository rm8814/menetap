## 2. Purpose

Menetap provides a curated hospitality booking and guest-service platform. The Partner authorizes Menetap to publish approved property information, room inventory, rates, policies, and selected services for guest discovery and booking.

Menetap may provide the Partner with guest communication, reservation support, marketing exposure, and operational escalation support powered by UPSCALE.

## 4. Partner obligations

The Partner agrees to:

1. Provide accurate, current, and complete property, room, rate, availability, photo, amenity, and policy information.
2. Maintain availability for at least the next 30 days during the pilot.
3. Honor confirmed reservations and published prices, subject to documented cancellation or force-majeure rules.
4. Notify Menetap promptly of closures, maintenance, changes in inventory, pricing, policies, or operating capacity.
5. Maintain reasonable cleanliness, safety, and service standards appropriate to the property category.
6. Respond promptly to reservation notifications and guest requests assigned to the Partner.
7. Handle hotel-related guest requests and property fulfillment.
8. Provide access to information reasonably needed to investigate guest complaints, booking issues, or service incidents.
9. Ensure that its staff and representatives follow applicable laws and do not discriminate unlawfully against guests.
10. Maintain any licenses, permits, insurance, and approvals required to operate the property.

## 5. Menetap obligations

Menetap agrees to:

1. Publish approved Partner information in a commercially reasonable manner.
2. Provide reservation notifications and relevant guest information.
3. Handle general guest communication through Menetap support during the pilot.
4. Coordinate with the Partner on hotel-related requests and reservation issues.
5. Coordinate escalations through the UPSCALE operations team where appropriate.
6. Apply agreed commission, payment, cancellation, and payout terms consistently.
7. Protect Partner information and personal data according to applicable law and Menetap’s privacy practices.

## 6. Inventory, rates, and featured placement

- Approved inventory will be recorded in the property data sheet or Menetap system.
- The Partner is responsible for the accuracy of room availability and rates supplied to Menetap.
- The initial minimum live inventory is one room type and one rate.
- The Partner must approve any material changes to public property information.
- Featured placement is subject to Menetap approval and the additional commission described below.

## 10. Guest services and third-party providers

Airport shuttle, train transfer, breakfast, early check-in, late check-out, travel insurance, and other add-ons may be fulfilled by the Partner or an approved third-party provider.

Each service must have defined:

- Provider and fulfillment owner
- Price and inclusions
- Availability and cutoff time
- Cancellation terms
- Guest instructions
- Incident and refund responsibility

## 11. Guest support and escalation

- Menetap support is the primary guest communication channel during the pilot.
- The Partner handles hotel-specific requests and on-property fulfillment.
- Transport providers handle transfer fulfillment.
- UPSCALE operations handles escalations and cross-party incidents.
- Urgent safety or welfare matters must be escalated immediately through the agreed emergency contacts.

## 12. Property content and intellectual property

The Partner grants Menetap a non-exclusive, revocable license to use property names, logos, descriptions, photographs, amenities, rates, and related content for listing, marketing, support, and booking purposes during the agreement.

The Partner confirms that it has the necessary rights to provide that content and that the content does not knowingly infringe third-party rights.

## 13. Data protection and confidentiality

Each Party must protect confidential business information and personal data received through the relationship. Personal data must be collected, accessed, used, retained, and shared only for legitimate booking, support, fulfillment, payment, safety, legal, or operational purposes.

The final agreement should include legally reviewed data-processing and cross-party data-sharing terms.

## 14. Quality, inspection, and suspension

Menetap may request updated information, conduct or arrange an inspection, pause a listing, or suspend bookings if:

- Information is materially inaccurate.
- Availability is repeatedly unreliable.
- Confirmed bookings are not honored.
- Cleanliness, safety, or service standards are materially below the agreed level.
- The Partner fails to respond to operational issues.
- There is suspected fraud, abuse, or legal non-compliance.

Menetap should provide notice and an opportunity to remedy issues where reasonably possible, except for urgent safety, fraud, or legal matters.
