# Menetap Greater Yogyakarta Partner Recruitment Tracker

## Pilot target

- Goal: 20 live properties within 30 days.
- Recruitment target: 25–30 qualified prospects.
- Coverage: broader Greater Yogyakarta.
- Property types: independent, boutique, budget, midscale, and upscale hotels; villas; guesthouses; homestays.
- Excluded for MVP: apartments.

## Status definitions

- **Prospect:** identified but not yet contacted.
- **Contacted:** first outreach sent or call attempted.
- **Interested:** partner has expressed interest.
- **Onboarding:** information and documents being collected.
- **Verification:** ownership/management and listing quality being checked.
- **Ready to publish:** all launch requirements complete except final publication.
- **Live:** meets the complete live-property definition.
- **Blocked:** progress depends on a known unresolved issue.
- **Declined:** partner is not participating.

## Live-property definition

A property is live only when:

- Property approved and verified.
- Listing published.
- At least one room type and rate available.
- Availability updated for the next 30 days.
- Partner trained on reservations and guest requests.
- Cancellation and payment terms configured.

## Partner acceptance checklist

### Verification

- [ ] Ownership or management contact verified.
- [ ] Correct property address and operating contact confirmed.
- [ ] Partner agreement accepted.
- [ ] Commission arrangement recorded.
- [ ] Payout/bank details collected securely.

### Listing quality

- [ ] Accurate property description.
- [ ] Accurate room descriptions and occupancy limits.
- [ ] Current photos received and approved.
- [ ] Amenities and facilities confirmed.
- [ ] Check-in/check-out times confirmed.
- [ ] House rules and important restrictions recorded.
- [ ] Cancellation terms configured.
- [ ] Total pricing reviewed for transparency.

### Inventory readiness

- [ ] At least one room type configured.
- [ ] At least one rate configured.
- [ ] Availability loaded for the next 30 days.
- [ ] Partner understands how to report changes.
- [ ] Test reservation completed.

### Training and launch

- [ ] Partner knows how new reservations are communicated.
- [ ] Partner knows how to respond to guest requests.
- [ ] Partner knows the escalation contact.
- [ ] Menetap support contact is confirmed.
- [ ] Property marked live.
