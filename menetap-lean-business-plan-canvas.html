Menetap

A hospitality platform that helps guests book complete stays and helps hotels operate, grow, and serve guests better.

menetap.powered by UPSCALE

Problem

  • Guests book rooms across fragmented platforms and arrange extras separately.
  • Hotels need demand, but also struggle with revenue, reservations, operations, and service quality.
  • Hospitality vendors lack efficient access to relevant hotel and guest demand.

Solution

  • One platform for stays, add-ons, and trip services.
  • One partner layer for distribution, operations, and hospitality services.
  • One vendor network for guest services, hotel services, and supplies.

Unique value proposition

Menetap connects the entire stay — from discovering a room to arranging what guests need before, during, and after their visit.

More than a room. Everything around the stay.
Transparent pricingComplete stayHotel growthLocal relevance

Unfair advantage

  • UPSCALE hospitality expertise and operational capability.
  • Connection between guest demand and hotel operations.
  • Integrated services create more value than room distribution alone.

Customer segments

  • Guests: travelers booking stays and services.
  • Partners: hotels, villas, and property operators.
  • Vendors: service and supply providers.

Channels

  • Menetap.com and web booking experience
  • Direct hotel and partner sales
  • Digital marketing, SEO, and content
  • Hotel and vendor referrals
  • Local hospitality network

Key metrics

  • Booking conversion and gross booking value
  • Add-on attach rate and average booking value
  • Active partners and partner retention
  • Vendor fulfillment and repeat usage
  • Guest satisfaction and support resolution time

Revenue streams

  • Accommodation booking commission
  • Commission or margin on guest add-ons
  • Partner subscriptions and service fees
  • Vendor transaction commissions
  • Hospitality supplies and professional services

Cost structure

  • Product, engineering, and platform infrastructure
  • Sales, marketing, and partner acquisition
  • Guest and partner support operations
  • Payment processing and transaction operations
  • Service quality, fulfillment, and vendor management

Strategic focus

Start with reliable hotel booking and a small number of high-value stay extensions — breakfast, transfers, early check-in, and rentals. Expand into partner operations and vendor commerce once the core booking loop is working.

Business model

Menetap is a multi-sided platform. Guest transactions create demand; hotel services deepen partner value; vendors expand the selection around every stay.

North-star outcome

Become the trusted infrastructure and commerce layer connecting guest demand, hotel inventory, hospitality operations, local services, and supplies.
