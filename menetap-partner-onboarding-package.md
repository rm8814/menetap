# Menetap Partner Onboarding Package

## 1. Partner proposition

Menetap helps hospitality businesses reach guests through a curated booking platform while supporting a better stay experience through transparent pricing, reliable reservations, and selected guest services.

During the pilot, Menetap and UPSCALE will support partners with listing setup, reservation communication, guest support, and escalation handling.

## 2. What Menetap provides

- Curated listing on Menetap.
- Property and room presentation support.
- Guest booking exposure through Menetap marketing and content.
- Guest communication and booking support.
- Reservation notifications.
- Support for selected add-on services.
- Operational escalation through UPSCALE.

## 3. Partner responsibilities

- Provide accurate property, room, rate, policy, and photo information.
- Keep availability accurate for at least the next 30 days.
- Honor confirmed reservations and published prices.
- Respond promptly to reservations and guest-related requests.
- Maintain agreed cleanliness and service standards.
- Notify Menetap promptly about closures, maintenance, or inventory changes.
- Handle hotel-specific requests and property fulfillment.

## 4. Commercial model

- Base commission: 10% per eligible hotel booking.
- Property-specific commission overrides may be agreed in writing.
- Featured property placement: additional 2% commission.
- Payment method during MVP: pay at hotel as the primary method.
- Manual bank transfer may be handled by Menetap where applicable.
- Payment gateway integration is not included in the MVP.

The final partner agreement should define the treatment of taxes, discounts, refunds, no-shows, manual bank transfers, and any add-on service commissions.

## 5. Information required from the partner

- Legal property name and public-facing name.
- Ownership or management contact.
- Property address and map location.
- Primary operational contact and escalation contact.
- Room types and occupancy limits.
- Rates and inclusions.
- Availability for the next 30 days.
- Check-in and check-out policies.
- Cancellation and no-show policies.
- Amenities and facilities.
- Accurate property and room photos.
- Bank and payout details through an approved secure process.

## 6. Onboarding process

1. Introductory conversation.
2. Property qualification.
3. Partner terms and commission confirmation.
4. Information and photo collection.
5. Ownership/management verification.
6. Listing and room configuration.
7. Pricing, cancellation, and payment setup.
8. 30-day availability setup.
9. Partner training.
10. Test reservation.
11. Final approval and publication.

## 7. Partner training agenda

- How Menetap reservations are received.
- How to confirm or flag a reservation issue.
- How to manage availability changes.
- How to respond to guest requests.
- Which requests Menetap handles.
- Which requests the hotel handles.
- How airport and train transfers are coordinated.
- How to escalate urgent issues to Menetap and UPSCALE.
- How cancellations, no-shows, and refunds are handled.

## 8. First outreach message

> Menetap is launching a curated hospitality platform in Greater Yogyakarta, powered by UPSCALE. We are inviting a small group of hotels, villas, guesthouses, and homestays to join our first pilot.
>
> Menetap helps properties reach guests through transparent pricing and curated listings, while our team supports guest communication and reservation operations. The initial partner model uses a 10% base commission, with no PMS or channel-manager integration required for the pilot.
>
> We would like to learn more about your property and explore whether it is a good fit for the first 20-property launch group.

## 9. Partner call questions

- What property types and room types are currently available?
- Who manages reservations and guest communication?
- How is availability currently updated?
- What are the busiest and slowest periods?
- What are the standard check-in and check-out times?
- Which breakfast, transfer, or early/late stay services are available?
- What would make Menetap valuable to your property?
- What operational concerns would prevent participation?
