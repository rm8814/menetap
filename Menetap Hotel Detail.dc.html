About this stay

A heritage-style villa tucked into the cool hills of Kaliurang, ten minutes from Merapi's base. Teak interiors, a private garden, and a kitchen staffed each morning with a Javanese breakfast. Quiet enough to work from, close enough to explore Borobudur and Prambanan in a day trip.
