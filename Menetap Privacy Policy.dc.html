Privacy policy

This policy explains what information Menetap collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

Account details (name, email, phone), booking history, payment method metadata (not full card numbers), and device/usage data when you use the site or app.

2. How we use it

To process bookings, run loyalty rewards, personalize search results, prevent fraud, and communicate booking-related updates.

3. Sharing

We share the minimum booking details needed with the property you book, and with payment processors to complete transactions. We do not sell your personal data.

4. Your choices

You can access, update, or request deletion of your data from Account Settings, or by contacting our support team via the Help center.

5. Data retention

We retain booking records as required for tax, legal, and dispute-resolution purposes, and delete other account data on request where not legally required to keep it.
