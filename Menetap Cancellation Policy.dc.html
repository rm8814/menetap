Cancellation policy

Each property sets its own cancellation policy, shown before you book and again on your confirmation page. Most listings fall into one of these three tiers:

PolicyFull refund if cancelledAfter that
FlexibleUp to 24 hours before check-inFirst night charged, remainder refunded
ModerateUp to 5 days before check-in50% refund up to 24 hours before, then no refund
StrictUp to 14 days before check-inNo refund inside 14 days

To cancel or modify a booking, go to My Trips, open the booking, and choose Cancel booking or Modify dates. Refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–10 business days. Menetap service fees are non-refundable except when a host cancels on you.
